# Structural sensitivity runs

These records explain why alternative model structures were introduced and how
they were evaluated. They are intended to preserve modelling judgment that is
not obvious from configuration diffs alone.

Keep the durable experimental record here:

- the question or observed representation problem;
- the structural change and comparison case;
- the configurations, assumptions, and expected artifacts;
- qualitative or quantitative findings; and
- the resulting modelling decision.

Keep generated Silver and Gold products, solver databases, logs, plots, and
validation reports in their normal local output directories. Link to their
manifests or stable run identities rather than committing large generated files.
