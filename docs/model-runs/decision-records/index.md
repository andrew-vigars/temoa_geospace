# Decision Records

Use a decision record when a modelling choice should remain understandable
after the originating analysis, branch or conversation is gone.

A record should state:

- the observed problem or decision pressure;
- the alternatives considered;
- the evidence or structural sensitivity used;
- the selected decision;
- consequences and known limitations; and
- links to configurations, manifests and related documentation.

Decision records explain the maintained model. Exploratory results that did not
lead to a decision remain structural-sensitivity records rather than accepted
model assumptions.
